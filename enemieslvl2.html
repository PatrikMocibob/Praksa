Animal

HP: 150
The Animal is a fast, low-threat creature that roams erratically around the map. Its actions are quite random and unpredictable, and it is just as likely to engage with players as it is to run away. While it can attack players directly, the attack is very slow and deals only 2 damage. It may be more beneficial to keep Animals alive, as they can trample Gnomes.

If a player attempts to pick an Animal up, it panics and runs away with increased speed. If two or more players grab an Animal at the same time, it will pause and play dead for a few seconds before spinning out of control.

The Animal prefers to run rather than fight, and is very easy to kill with any weapon due to its lack of defensive abilities.

The Animal will occasionally collide with objects around the map, damaging itself in the process.
Takes 4 strength upgrades to pick up.

Rugrat (Baby Monster)

HP: 150
The Rugrat roams around grabbing valuable items and, if it sees a player while holding something, it throws the item at them, dealing damage to both the player and the item.

If it’s empty-handed, it runs away instead. It has no other attacks, but don’t underestimate it—it’s small but strong, capable of lifting and throwing two player items at once, which can one-shot a player, the item, or both.

It can be killed by smashing it against the floor or walls, but it’s much heavier than Gnomes, needing either 2 people or one person with 4 strength upgrades to lift. If you’re unarmed or alone, these little thieves can be a real problem.

When holding it up, you have time to smash it around 2 times before it jumps out of your hands and run away, either to your cart or completely away.
If the player grabs the item while the Rugrat is still holding it, it is possible to "Catch" the item.

Upscream

HP: 50
The Upscream is a deformed humanoid that crawls on the floor. When it spots a player, it grabs them, screams, and flings them backwards, dealing 10 damage and stunning them for 2 seconds, often grabbing you right away again dealing more and more damage, until the player manages to flee. The player will likely receive additional damage due to physics damage. The pits are the most dangerous in this case, since from such a strong push you are very likely to fall there.

While a single Upscream is manageable, multiple can be deadly as they can stun-lock a player in an endless loop until they die. If there’s more than one, make sure your team is coordinated or it’s game over.

The hammer is the best weapon due to their small size, but a Tranquilizer Gun works too, leaving them immobile for a few seconds—at which point you can grab them and treat them like a gnome (which means slamming them into the ground).
Takes 4 strength upgrades to pick up.

Bowtie

HP: 100
The Bowtie is a large, puffy monster that roams at a medium pace and becomes a problem once it spots a player. When it does, it lets out a continuous scream, pushing the player back and disabling sprinting and jumping until they escape.

The scream itself doesn’t deal damage, but if it pushes you into a wall or obstacle, it hurts badly. It can also push small monsters like Rugrats, Bangers, and Gnomes, occasionally causing chaos.

Sometimes, Bowties will randomly enter a frenzy, stomping around at high speed and damaging everything in their way. Luckily, they are slow when roaming and can’t move while screaming, making them easy targets.

Tranquilizer Guns and firearms work well, but melee weapons are just as effective. Takes 9 strength upgrades to pick up.

Chef (Frog)

HP: 150
The Chef is a slow-moving enemy that resembles a frog in a chef's uniform. Upon spotting a player it pauses, smiles, and then leaps at the player while swinging its large cleavers wildly.

Players are able to sidestep the Chef's lunge attack, as once it has drawn its knives the direction of its leap is locked. Alternatively, hiding behind a door or wall is also effective.

The Chef deals a lot of damage when it attacks, but not enough to kill a player with full HP in one leap. If the Chef misses its attack, it ragdolls and flails about on the ground for a short time.
Takes 9 strength upgrades to pick up.

Hidden

HP: 100
The Hidden is a nearly-invisible monster with only a black smoke cloud for a head and faint footprints as signs of its presence. If it touches a player, it screams, grabs them, and carries them around at high speed, stunning them and forcing them to drop their items.

It’s hard to spot, so you might not even realize one is in the level until it snatches you. It can drag you straight into other enemies or off the map. The easiest way to avoid being grabbed is to listen for its breathing and run for cover.

The Hidden leaves smoky black footsteps. If you're fast, you can grab it or swing a bat to hit. Fans can knock it down, and once grounded, it can be picked up and slammed.
It can also help in late-game by carrying players back to the truck. Wild, right?

Banger

HP: 65
The Banger is an explosive enemy that spawns in groups of three and wanders aimlessly. If it sees a player or takes damage, its fuse ignites, and it charges at the nearest player.

Once lit, the fuse cannot be put out. The Banger kills any player at close proximity and deals high damage from a distance. Bangers can be picked up and thrown like grenades, but they break free after ~3 seconds.

Listen for their fuse sounds and spread out to avoid chain kills. They only have 3 HP, so smashing them twice then tossing them is ideal.
Great for turning enemy mobs into craters.

Mentalist (Alien)

HP: 150
The Mentalist is a floating enemy that searches for players and, when it finds one, activates an anti-gravity aura that lifts all players and items nearby, holding them immobile for 10 seconds. After that, the aura turns red, and everything gets slammed back down, often killing players trapped under furniture due to physics damage.

You can also save your friends by dragging them out — safest to do this in the short window when the aura becomes red. If lifted high up, though, it just counts as fall damage (40-50 HP loss), which can be prevented by placing a pocket cart below to cushion your fall.

It can teleport anywhere at will, flipping mid-air as it disappears, making it hard to track. Killing it is fairly easy with melee weapons due to low HP, but if caught, have a teammate ready to pull you away before it drops you.

Hiding is the safest bet, as it's deaf with poor eyesight — but if spotted, it chases at a medium pace. Since it can teleport, always assume it’s waiting behind the next door.
Takes 4 strength upgrades to pick up.
