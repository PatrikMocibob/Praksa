Headman (Floating Head)

HP: 250
The Headman is a floating giant head that roams around, making loud biting noises as it looks for players. If it spots a player, it screams, charges, and bites, dealing 50 damage per chomp plus possible knockback damage.

If it starts chasing you, just turn a corner and hide—it loses interest fast. Fighting it? Wait for it to attack, since it has a long cooldown. Its poor reach makes sledgehammers a great choice, and guns work well thanks to its massive hitbox. The Baseball Bat is also effective due to its knockback.

Takes 13 strength upgrades to pick up.

Huntsman

HP: 250
The Huntsman is a blind hunter that uses his hunting shotgun as a cane. He is slow, silent, and deadly. One shot deals 100 damage. He hears everything: footsteps, object movement, even text/voice chat.

He will fire at any sound source, and if he touches a player, he fires instantly. Hide in furniture or at high ground to stay safe. Throw objects to distract him, dodge when he grunts, or bait a shot and run while he reloads.

He cannot descend stairs safely, and is easy to lift when stunned. Baseball Bat works well up close.

Takes 9 strength upgrades to pick up.

Robe

HP: 250
The Robe silently follows players, then knocks them back for 50–100 damage depending on their stance. Looking at its mask enrages it, increasing its speed dramatically.

It remembers hiding spots and will reach under cover. If it doesn’t see you or get touched by anything you hold, it may ignore you. Best strategy: stay out of sight and don’t engage.

Takes 13 strength upgrades to pick up.

Clown (Beamer)

HP: 250
The Clown uses a laser beam that deals 30–70 damage depending on proximity and hits multiple times. After a beam, it cools down for 8 seconds. During cooldown, it may kick players for 10–50 damage total.

The beam targets your head—crouch or hide behind cover to survive. It's vulnerable after firing. High ledges and quick strikes work well. Avoid scrap loss by keeping valuables far away.

Takes 13 strength upgrades to pick up.

Reaper

HP: 250
The Reaper is slow and deaf with poor vision. If it sees you, it charges with arm windmills, damaging anything nearby. It can’t hear voices or footsteps but does detect the gravi-beam.

Crouching generally avoids detection. Hiding under shallow objects won't work—it can crouch and strike. Deep cover or stealth is key. Don’t fight unless you have backup.

Takes 9 strength upgrades to pick up.

Trudge

HP: 500
The Trudge drags a huge mace and makes loud grinding sounds. Upon spotting players, it slams the mace to pull them in, then bashes for 100 damage. The slam is instant death if you’re directly in front of it.

Avoid tight spaces. Hide far away, preferably in another room. It’s slow and easy to outrun, but very tanky. Landmines and traps work due to its predictable movement.

Takes 13 strength upgrades to pick up.
